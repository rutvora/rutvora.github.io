I am Rut Vora, a software engineer interested in Systems and Networks, especially Systems and Networks Security.

I currently work at Arista Networks on the PCIe subsystem of their switches.  

Before joining Arista, I was a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia, advised by [Prof. Aastha Mehta](https://aasthakm.github.io/).  
My thesis is titled "Side channels in networks: from the internet to interconnects" and is available [here](https://open.library.ubc.ca/soa/cIRcle/collections/ubctheses/24/items/1.0448228).

I started my career in networks in the [Office of the CTO](https://www.f5.com/company/octo) at [F5, Inc.](https://f5.com).  
There, I created various proof-of-concepts for accelerating F5 products. My works spanned technologies like eBPF, DPDK, P4 and WASM.
For details, you can check [my resume](https://public.rutvora.com/Resume_Rut_Vora.pdf).

I completed my undergraduate in Computer Science from BITS Pilani in India (2016-2020).
